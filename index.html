Support & Privacy Policy

Support

Fastpitch Allstars is a fastpitch softball game built for players ages 10–16. If something isn't working — a purchase that didn't arrive, a crash, or a bug on the field — email us and we'll help you out.

Common Questions

I bought coins but didn't get them

Open the Shop, tap Get Coins, then Restore Purchases. Coins are added back automatically. If that doesn't work, email us with the date of the purchase.

How do I get more coins?

You earn coins just by playing — games, challenges, and Softball IQ quizzes all pay out. Coin packs are optional and never required to progress.

Is the game okay for kids?

Yes. There's no chat, no ads, no user-generated content, and no data collection. Coin purchases are behind a confirmation, and we recommend parents enable Apple's Ask to Buy.

I found a bug

Email us with what happened and what device you're using. Screenshots help a lot.

Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 7, 2026

The short version

We don't collect any personal information. Fastpitch Allstars has no accounts, no sign-up, no analytics, and no advertising.

Data the game stores

Your career progress, coins, and settings are saved only on your own device. They never leave it, and deleting the app deletes them.

Purchases

Optional coin packs are processed by Apple and by RevenueCat, our payment service provider, acting on Apple's platform. RevenueCat processes purchase receipts solely to validate and restore your purchases; it does not receive your name or contact details from us. Apple's own privacy policy governs App Store payments.

Children's privacy

Because we collect no data at all, no data is collected from children. The game complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA).

Contact about privacy
